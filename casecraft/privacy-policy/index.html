CaseCraft

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

CaseCraft is a single-player detective puzzle game for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, developed by Hoffmann Tech LTDA (“we”, “us”) and published as Magic Lab Solutions. It is built to collect as little as it can: what it needs to know which worlds you have bought, and anonymous information about how the game is played. CaseCraft has no user accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

Your cases stay on your device

Your progress — the cases you have started and solved, the suspects you placed, your notes, hints and timers — and your choices in Settings (language, downloads over mobile data, sound, haptics and the other play options) are stored on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Worlds you download

London 1964 comes with the app. Other worlds are downloaded from Apple’s servers when you ask for them (Apple’s On-Demand Resources), under Apple’s terms. Nothing about you is sent to us when a world downloads.

Purchases

London is free. Every other world can be bought on its own, or all of them, including new worlds as they arrive, with the All Worlds pass: monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. The monthly and yearly passes renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple Account settings. Apple processes every payment through your Apple Account; we never receive card details, your name or your Apple Account email address.

To know what you have bought, on this device and your others, the app contacts our own purchase service, Horse Revenue, which runs on Google Cloud. It receives a random identifier created for this installation, the app version, the operating system version and the App Store country, together with the purchase record signed by Apple (the product, dates, price and transaction identifiers). Apple also sends us notices about each purchase, such as renewals and refunds. We use this information to provide and restore your purchases and to keep financial records. It is never used for advertising or tracking, and never combined with anything that identifies you.

Deleting the app removes the random identifier from the device; purchase records are kept only as long as needed for support, accounting and legal obligations.

How the game is played

The app also sends anonymous usage information to the same service, under the same random identifier: when a session starts and how long it lasts, and when the offer for the worlds is shown, which plan is chosen, and whether it is bought or closed. We use it to understand how the game is played and how the offer performs. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never combined with anything that identifies you. The app also reads settings from the same service (for example how many cases of a world can be tried before buying); that request carries only the random identifier and the app version.

Controllers and keyboards

Touch, keyboard, mouse, Siri Remote and game-controller input is handled on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

CaseCraft does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The purchase records described above contain no names or contact details.

Your choices

You can delete the app at any time to remove everything it stored on your device. To ask about the purchase records linked to your installation, write to us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above.
