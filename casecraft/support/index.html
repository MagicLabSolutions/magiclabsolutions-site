Support

Help with CaseCraft on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV

Which worlds are free?

London 1964 and its 64 cases are free and come with the app. Every other world can be bought on its own, or all of them, including new worlds as they arrive, with the All Worlds pass (monthly, yearly or lifetime).

I bought something on another device

Open Settings › Purchases › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Cancel or change a pass

Monthly and yearly passes are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the pass stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A world does not open

Worlds other than London are downloaded when you first open them. Check that you are online, then open Downloads from the home screen; Settings shows how much space each world uses and can free it again. Your progress is kept when a world is removed.

A puzzle seems wrong

Every case is checked by a solver to have exactly one solution. If one still looks wrong, write to us with the world and the case number (for example LDN-12) and we will look at it.
