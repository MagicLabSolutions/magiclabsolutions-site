Starwake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Starwake is a single-player game developed by Hoffmann Tech LTDA (“we”, “us”), published as MagicLab Solutions. It is built to collect as little as it can: only what the full game needs to know what you have bought.

What we collect

Starwake has no user accounts, no advertising, no trackers and no analytics SDKs. The only information that leaves your device for us is what the full game needs, described below.

The full game and purchases

Starwake is free to play through its prologue at Cairn and the rest of the Harrow system. Crossing to other stars opens with a one-time purchase of the full game, or a free 3-day trial. Apple processes every payment through your Apple Account; we never receive card details, your name or your Apple Account email address.

To know what you have bought, on this device and your others, the app contacts our own purchase service, Horse Revenue, which runs on Google Cloud. It receives a random identifier created for this installation, the app version, the operating system version and the App Store country, together with the purchase record signed by Apple (the product, dates, price and transaction identifiers), and a note each time the full game’s card is shown or closed. Apple also sends us notices about each purchase, such as refunds. We use this information to provide and restore your purchases, to see how the full game’s card performs and to keep financial records. It is never used for advertising or tracking, and never combined with anything that identifies you.

Deleting the app removes the random identifier from the device; purchase records are kept only as long as needed for support, accounting and legal obligations.

Your game saves

Your progress — including the name you give your surveyor — is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your save is also kept in your own private iCloud database, so the same galaxy is there on your other Apple devices. That copy lives in your private iCloud storage — we cannot see or access it — and is handled under Apple’s iCloud terms and Apple’s Privacy Policy. Signing out of iCloud, or turning iCloud off for Starwake in Settings, stops it.

Your settings

Your choices in Settings — captions, handedness, haptics, sound and music — are stored on your device and never leave it.

Controllers and keyboards

Touch, keyboard, mouse and game-controller input is handled on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

Starwake does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The purchase records described above contain no names or contact details.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@magiclabsolutions.com.
