Starwake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Starwake is a single-player game developed by Hoffmann Tech LTDA (“we”, “us”), published as MagicLab Solutions. It is built to collect nothing about you.

We don’t collect your data

Starwake does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs.

Your game saves

Your progress — including the name you give your surveyor — is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your save is also kept in your own private iCloud database, so the same galaxy is there on your other Apple devices. That copy lives in your private iCloud storage — we cannot see or access it — and is handled under Apple’s iCloud terms and Apple’s Privacy Policy. Signing out of iCloud, or turning iCloud off for Starwake in Settings, stops it.

Your settings

Your choices in Settings — captions, handedness, haptics, sound and music — are stored on your device and never leave it.

Controllers and keyboards

Touch, keyboard, mouse and game-controller input is handled on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

Starwake does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@magiclabsolutions.com.
