# New website icon directions — 2026-09-30

The repositories for Hortulus and Moonfold contained AppIcon entries without image filenames.
These website identities were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, visually
inspected, and delivered as 1024px PNG masters plus optimized WebP display assets. They have
not been installed into the native apps' asset catalogs.

## Hortulus

1024 square, full bleed warm cream #F3EEE2; a mature botanical sprig with three bold moss
#4E6B3A and sage #9DB39A leaves in a minimal terracotta #C0643B pot. Restrained paper relief,
strong silhouette at small sizes, no writing, numbers, watermark, external border, glass or
photorealism. The living-herbarium identity from the repository is the reference.

Master: `images/portfolio/hortulus/icon-1024.png`.

## Moonfold

1024 square, full bleed ink blue #18233A; a large warm cream folded-paper crescent cradling
an open ivory and peach storybook, with at most two tiny amber stars. Quiet, refined cut-paper
relief for a family bedtime ritual, no text, watermark, external border or glass.

Master: `images/portfolio/moonfold/icon-1024.png`.

## WhyMe

No icon was invented: the repository contains only its name. The site uses the studio mark
until the owner provides the product purpose. Its preparation page is excluded from indexing.
