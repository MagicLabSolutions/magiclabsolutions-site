# Header and search artwork across Apple platforms

Prepared for the owner's October 13, 2026 request. The package covers the 17
MagicLab catalog apps that have an App Store Connect record, across 31 registered
Apple app/platform combinations. The two Heroes apps found in the shared account
are outside the catalog and await the owner's scope clarification.

`manifest.json` contains 2,124 platform/language/placement records. Approved
universal headers are reused without changing their pixels. Native Mac and Vision
Pro search artwork uses verified captures. Game/platform combinations without a
native capture use explicitly platform-neutral approved brand art, with no simulated
interface. Brainfold's Simplified Chinese listing uses the approved schoolbook/PDF
concept without inventing a localized native screen.

Opaque full-resolution PNG masters remain in the ignored `exports/` directory;
editable rendering source, input evidence, records and optimized review images
are tracked. `index.html` is a local review with app/platform/language selectors.
The render, delivery, review and audit scripts live in `scripts/marketing/`.

The preview and integrity validation covers every record. The browser review checks
31 platform combinations at 320px and 1440px. Captured app images and retained
TumTum/Soooon screenshot collections are unchanged; Sundust's existing preview
video order is checked in the final delivery audit.

Delivery uses each platform's current version independently. If a platform has no
editable or review version, the authorized next minor manual-release draft is
created. Active App Reviews are preserved; images can still be uploaded to their
independent Asset Library. Existing placements and images are backed up before a
replacement. Source files are frozen during upload, and every assigned image is
read back from Apple. No binary or review submission is performed.

Final upload/placement coverage and remaining review locks are recorded by the
read-only `audit/final-verification.json`, once delivery has finished. Upload,
draft placement, App Review approval and public display are separate outcomes.
Apple currently documents these creative formats as displayed on iOS/iPadOS 27
or later, even when App Store Connect accepts a draft binding on another platform.

[Apple asset management](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-your-app-store-assets/)
