Toc Toc is an app for Mac and iPhone that knocks right before your meetings. This page explains,
in plain language, what it reads, what it keeps, what it sends and what it never does. The short
version: **your meetings never leave your device.** The only thing Toc Toc sends anywhere is what
it needs to know whether you have Toc Toc Pro.

### What Toc Toc reads

With your permission, Toc Toc reads the events on the calendars of your Mac or iPhone (iCloud,
Google, Exchange, Outlook and others), through Apple's EventKit. For each event in the
next 24 hours it looks at the title, the start and end, the calendar it belongs to, whether you
accepted or declined, how many other people are invited, and the location, notes and link —
only to decide when to knock and to find the call you can join.

This happens on your device, in the app's memory. Toc Toc **never** creates, changes or deletes an
event, and never copies your events anywhere.

---

### What Toc Toc keeps on your device

* Your **settings**: when to knock, which calendars are on, snooze length, the apps that must not
  be interrupted, the card's appearance, a pause you set.
* Which reminders you already **joined, snoozed or closed**, so the same one does not come back.
  These entries are removed a day after the meeting ends.
* Whether you have **Toc Toc Pro**, as Horse Revenue last confirmed it (see below), so the app
  works without a connection, and the random identifier described there.

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your device, and disappears when you delete the app.

### What leaves your device

Your meetings never do. Toc Toc has **no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**.

* **Toc Toc Pro**: Apple sells Toc Toc Pro through the App Store, and we never receive your
  payment details or your Apple Account. To know on each of your devices whether you have Pro,
  Toc Toc uses **Horse Revenue**, MagicLab's own purchase service, which runs on Google Cloud in
  the United States. When the app starts or comes to the front, and when you buy or restore, it
  sends Horse Revenue:
  * a random identifier the app creates on your device, which is not linked to your name, your
    email or your Apple Account;
  * the App Store's signed records of your Toc Toc Pro purchases, if you have any: which plan,
    when, the price and currency, and Apple's transaction numbers;
  * the app's version, your system's version and your App Store country.

  Apple also tells Horse Revenue when a subscription starts, renews, is refunded or ends. Horse
  Revenue uses these records only to unlock Pro on your devices and to count our sales, free
  trials and renewals. It never receives anything about your calendars or your meetings.

* **Joining a call**: when you press Join, the meeting's link opens in its app (Zoom, Teams…) or
  in your browser. From there, that service's own privacy policy applies.
* **Crash reports and statistics**: if you chose to share analytics with app developers in your
  device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and aggregate numbers with us. We use
  them only to fix problems.

### Your choices

* Turn calendar access off at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars on
  the Mac, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars on the iPhone; Toc Toc then stays silent.
* Switch individual calendars off in Toc Toc's settings, under Calendars.
* Delete the app to delete everything it kept on your device. To ask about your purchase records
  in Horse Revenue, write to [contact@magiclabsolutions.com](mailto:contact@magiclabsolutions.com).
