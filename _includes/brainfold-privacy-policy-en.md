Brainfold turns the pages you scan or the PDFs you import into flashcards, quizzes, summaries and
an AI tutor. This page explains, in plain language, what the app stores, where it goes, who
processes it, and how to delete it. It applies to Brainfold on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

---

### Your account

You can start without an account ("Try it — no account needed"): the app then creates an
anonymous account so your packs can be saved. You can later link it, or sign in, with **Sign in
with Apple**, **Google** or an **email and password**. Sign-in is handled by Firebase
Authentication; we never see your password.

For an account we keep your **display name**, your **email** (when there is one), a profile photo
link if your sign-in provider gives one, your **language**, **time zone**, **appearance setting**,
your **plan** and its renewal dates, your **study streak**, and a **device identifier** used only
to stop the free welcome credits from being claimed again by reinstalling the app.

---

### What you study

* **Scans and PDFs.** The pages you photograph or import are uploaded so our servers can read
  them. Uploads are deleted as soon as the pack is ready. A copy of the original stays on your
  device so you can open it again.
* **Study packs.** The text read from your pages, and the flashcards, quiz questions, summaries,
  chapter names and groups made from it, are stored with your account so they sync between your
  devices.
* **Progress.** Your answers, scores, review schedule and study time, to power spaced repetition
  and your insights.
* **AI tutor chats.** Your messages to the tutor and its replies, stored with the pack.
* **Battles.** When you host or join a battle, the other players in that room see your display
  name, your answers' results and your score.
* **Credits.** Your credit balance and a history of what each AI action cost.

The demo pack you can try before signing up is bundled with the app: nothing about it leaves your
device.

### Who processes your data

We use a small number of services to run Brainfold. None of them may use your data for their own
purposes, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

* **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Remote Config,
  Analytics, Crashlytics): accounts, storage of your packs, our servers, app settings, anonymous
  usage statistics and crash reports.
* **Google Gemini API**: reads your scans and PDFs and writes the flashcards, quizzes and
  summaries.
* **Anthropic Claude API**: answers your questions in the AI tutor.
* **Horse Revenue** (our own purchase service), **Apple App Store** and **Google Play**: handle
  subscriptions, credit top-ups and purchase records. We never see your card details.

The AI providers receive only the content needed to answer a request (the text or images of your
pages, or your tutor conversation), not your name or email, and do not use it to train their
models under our agreements with them.

---

### Analytics and crash reports

Brainfold records events such as "a pack was created" or "a quiz was finished", and
crash reports, to understand what works and to fix problems. They are linked to a random app
instance ID and your account's random user ID, never to your name or email, and are never used
for advertising. Brainfold shows no ads and does not
track you across other companies' apps or websites.

---

### How long we keep it

* Uploaded scans and PDFs: until the pack is made, then deleted.
* Your account, packs, progress and chats: until you delete them or your account.
* Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
* Usage statistics: up to 14 months; crash reports: up to 90 days.

---

### Deleting your data

You can delete a single pack from its menu at any time. To delete your account and everything
stored with it, open **Settings → Delete account** in the app, or email us. Details:
[Delete your Brainfold account](/brainfold/delete-account/).

---

### Children

Brainfold is meant for students aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from
children under 13; if you believe a child has used Brainfold, write to us and we will delete the
account.

---

### Where the data lives and your rights

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers (United States) and may be processed in other
countries where our providers operate, with safeguards that comply with the GDPR and Brazil's LGPD.
You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its processing, at
[contact@magiclabsolutions.com](mailto:contact@magiclabsolutions.com). You may also complain to
your local data protection authority.
