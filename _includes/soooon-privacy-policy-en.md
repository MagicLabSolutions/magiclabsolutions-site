Soooon is a countdown app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, provided by Magic Lab Solutions. This policy explains what Soooon keeps on your device, what it reads only with your permission, what its plans send to our subscription service, and how to reach us.

## Your countdowns stay on your device

Your countdowns, your settings and the places and traditions you choose are stored on your device, in a space shared only with Soooon's own widgets and Live Activities. We never receive them. Soooon has no account, no ads, no advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

## Contacts

If you allow it, Soooon reads the names and birthdays in your contacts so you can count down to them. This happens on your device; nothing from your contacts is uploaded or sent to us. You can turn this access off at any time in your device's settings.

## Calendar and Reminders

If you allow it, Soooon reads the names, dates and times of the events in your calendars and of the reminders you have given a due date, so you can count down to them. It only reads: it never adds, changes or deletes an event or a reminder. This happens on your device, and nothing from your calendars or reminders is uploaded or sent to us. A countdown made from an event or a reminder remembers which one it came from, on your device, so it can move when the event or the due date moves. You can turn this access off at any time in your device's settings.

## Notifications and Live Activities

Notifications, the little sounds that come with them and Live Activities are scheduled on your device by iOS and macOS. Nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in Soooon's settings or in your device's settings.

## Share cards and Photos

Share cards are made on your device and go only where you choose to send them. If you save one, Soooon asks for permission to add it to your photo library; it only adds images and never reads your library. An invitation to count down together carries the countdown's name, date and emoji, and the name you sign it with, inside the link you send; it goes only where you send it, and we never receive it.

## Plans and purchases

Soooon is free with every holiday and three countdowns of your own; Soooon Pro, offered as a yearly or monthly subscription or a one-time purchase, unlocks the rest. Apple processes every payment through your Apple Account, and we never receive card details, your name or your Apple Account email address. When Soooon shows its plans or checks your plan, it contacts our subscription service, Horse Revenue, which runs on Google Cloud. For this, the service receives a random identifier created for this installation, the app version, the operating system version and the App Store country, together with the purchase records signed by Apple (the product, dates, price and transaction identifiers). Like any server, it also receives the IP address and the user agent of each request, and keeps the latest of them with that record. Apple also sends us notices about each plan, such as renewals, cancellations and refunds. We use this information to provide and support plans, to decide which plans to offer, and to keep financial records. It is never used for advertising or tracking, and it never includes your countdowns. Deleting the app removes the random identifier from your device; purchase records are kept only as long as needed for support, accounting and legal obligations. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store account settings.

## When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you include. We use it to reply and resolve your request, and keep the correspondence only as long as needed for support and applicable obligations. Our email provider processes messages to deliver this service. You can ask us to access, correct or delete information in support correspondence.

## Children

Soooon is for everyone and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## These web pages and Apple services

The privacy and terms links in the app open this website only when you select them. These pages contain no advertising tags, analytics scripts, forms or cookies set by us. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, receives technical request information such as an IP address when a page is loaded, and its privacy statement applies to that hosting. Apple separately operates the App Store and optional diagnostics under its own policies.

## Your rights and questions

For privacy questions or requests about information we receive, email [contact@magiclabsolutions.com](mailto:contact@magiclabsolutions.com). Your rights under applicable data-protection law remain available, including the right to raise a concern with the relevant authority.

## Changes

If Soooon's data practices change, we will update this page and its date and provide any notice or choices required for the change.
