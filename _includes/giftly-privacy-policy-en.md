Giftly remembers the people you care about, their dates and the gifts that suit them, and runs
Secret Santa draws. This page explains, in plain language, what the app keeps, where it goes, who
processes it, and how to delete it. It applies to Giftly on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

### No account

Giftly has no sign-up and no login. Most of what you type never reaches us.

---

### What stays on your device

* **Your people.** Names, birthdays and other dates, tastes, sizes, gift ideas, gifts you gave,
  notes, groups and photos are stored on your device.
* **iPhone, iPad and Mac.** If iCloud is on, Giftly keeps your devices in step through **your own
  private iCloud** (Apple's CloudKit private database). We cannot read it.
* **Android.** Your data stays on the device; there is no sync between devices.
* **Contacts.** When you import from Contacts, Giftly reads the contacts you pick (name, birthday,
  other dates and photo) on your device. None of a contact's data is sent to us.
* **Reminders and widgets** are prepared on your device.
* **Pass-the-phone Secret Santa** lives on the organiser's device (and their private iCloud), like
  your people.

---

### Sharing people

When you share people by link or QR code, their details travel inside the link itself, after the
`#`. Browsers do not send that part to any server, so the list goes from you to the person you
send it to without passing through us. Our page that opens these links has no analytics.

---

### Online Secret Santa

When an organiser chooses **"Everyone joins on their own phone"**, the exchange is stored online
so each person can join from their own phone with the invite link:

* **Stored:** the exchange's name, day and maximum budget, the organiser's name, the names of the
  people taking part, each person's **wish list and tastes** (only what they choose to write), and
  the result of the draw.
* **Who can see what:** anyone with the invite link sees the exchange and the list of names, so
  they can pick theirs. A person's wish list can be read by that person and, after the draw, by
  whoever drew them. The organiser's phone makes the draw and keeps only the organiser's own match;
  every other match can be read only by the person who drew it.
* **Identity:** joining creates a random anonymous identifier for your device (Firebase Anonymous
  Authentication). No name, email or phone number is asked for.
* **How long:** everything is deleted **30 days after the gift swap** (or 90 days after the
  exchange was created if it has no date), and immediately when the organiser deletes it.

---

### Who processes your data

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. These services act on our behalf:

* **Google Firebase** (Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore, United States): the online
  Secret Santa only.
* **Horse Revenue** (our own purchase and analytics service, on Google Cloud): verifies Giftly Pro
  purchases and receives the anonymous usage events described below.
* **Apple App Store** and **Google Play**: handle payments for Giftly Pro and the rating prompt.
  We never see your card details.
* **Apple iCloud**: your private sync on Apple devices, under your Apple Account.

---

### Analytics

Giftly records anonymous events such as "a person was added", "a gift idea was checked" or "a
screen was opened", to understand what helps and what to improve. They carry counts, on/off flags
and fixed words only: **never names, dates, notes, ideas or anything you type**, and they are tied
to a random app ID, not to you. Giftly shows no ads and does not track you across other
companies' apps or websites.

---

### Deleting your data

* Delete a person, an idea or a Secret Santa in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes what
  is on that device; on Apple devices, iCloud data can be removed in your device's iCloud settings.
* An online Secret Santa is deleted when its organiser deletes it, and automatically after the
  periods above.
* For anything else, write to [contact@magiclabsolutions.com](mailto:contact@magiclabsolutions.com).

---

### Children

Giftly is meant for adults planning gifts. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal data from them; if you believe a child has used the online Secret
Santa, write to us and we will delete it.

may process data in other countries with safeguards that comply with the GDPR and Brazil's LGPD.
You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its processing, at
[contact@magiclabsolutions.com](mailto:contact@magiclabsolutions.com). You may also complain to
your local data protection authority.
