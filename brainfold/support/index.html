{{ s.group_account | default: "Account & syncing" }}

{{ s.q_account | default: "Do I need an account?" }}

{{ s.a_account_1 | default: "No — you can tap “Try it — no account needed” and start scanning straight away." }}

{{ s.a_account_2 | default: "An account is what makes your study packs follow you: it backs your work up and syncs it to your other devices. Without one, everything lives only on the device you created it on, and it goes away with the app." }}

{{ s.q_link | default: "I started without an account — can I keep what I made?" }}

{{ s.a_link | default: "Yes. Open Settings → Link Account and sign in with Apple or with an email and password. Your existing packs, cards and progress move over to the account rather than being replaced. Do this before you delete the app or switch phones — an unlinked guest session cannot be recovered afterwards." }}

{{ s.q_signin | default: "Which sign-in options are there?" }}

{{ s.a_signin | default: "Sign in with Apple, or an email address and password. Google sign-in appears in the app but is not switched on yet — if you tap it you'll see a message saying so. If you forgot your password, use “Forgot password?” on the email screen and we'll send a reset link." }}

{{ s.q_sync | default: "My packs aren't on my other device" }}

{{ s.a_sync | default: "Make sure you're signed in to the same account on both devices — guest sessions don't sync. If you are signed in and something is still missing, the device may have been offline when you created it; open the app with a connection and give it a moment." }}

{{ s.group_credits | default: "Credits" }}

{{ s.q_credits | default: "How do credits work?" }}

{{ s.a_credits_1 | default: "Credits are what the AI work costs — scanning pages and generating flashcards, quizzes, summaries and tutor replies. The app shows the cost before you confirm, so nothing is ever spent by surprise." }}

{{ s.a_credits_2 | default: "Your monthly allowance resets on the 1st. Paid plans also top up a smaller amount every day at midnight. Credits sit in buckets with their own expiry dates, and the oldest ones are always used first — Settings shows your balance, the next refresh, and anything about to expire." }}

{{ s.q_out_of_credits | default: "I've run out of credits" }}

{{ s.a_out_of_credits | default: "You can wait for the next refresh, buy a top-up, or move to a plan with a bigger allowance. Everything you have already generated stays readable and studiable while you're out — only new AI work needs credits." }}

{{ s.q_numbers | default: "How many credits does my plan include?" }}

{{ s.a_numbers | default: "Open the plans screen in the app for the current numbers. We tune allowances and costs from time to time, so the app is always the accurate source rather than this page." }}

{{ s.q_restore | default: "I paid but the app still shows me as free" }}

{{ s.a_restore | default: "Tap “Restore Purchases” on the plans screen, and check you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the receipt and we'll sort it out." }}

{{ s.a_manage | default: "Settings → Manage subscription takes you straight to Apple's subscription screen, where upgrades, downgrades and cancellation all happen. {{ s.q_refund | default: "Can I get a refund?" }}

{{ s.group_using | default: "Using Brainfold" }}

{{ s.q_scan | default: "What can I scan?" }}

{{ s.a_scan | default: "Photos of textbook pages, handwritten notes, printouts, and PDFs. Good light and a flat page make a real difference to what the AI can read. How many pages you can put through in one go depends on your plan." }}

{{ s.q_quality | default: "The flashcards it made aren't very good" }}

{{ s.q_battle | default: "How does Battle Mode work?" }}
