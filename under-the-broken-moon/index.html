Under the Broken Moon

A shelter-building survival game for iPhone and iPad

The moon broke forty-one years ago, and its pieces still fall. The sky kills, but the rock holds, so you dig.

Arrive alone with a shovel, a hatch and a few days of canned food. Dig down, wire a generator, find water and grow food. Strangers knock on the hatch and you decide who comes in. Give each of them a job, and keep them alive through meteor showers, storms, floods, raiders, wolves and whatever lives in the deep caves.

Download on the App Store

What you'll do

Build room by room in cross-section: generators, farms, kitchens, bunks, washrooms and med bays, joined by ladders and fed by power cables and water pipes. Every survivor has a name, a trait and a mood; they make friends, fall in love, argue and grieve. Find old-world bunkers, a buried gas station and a lost temple, and hold three sites, Dry Creek, Saltmarsh and Old Metro, each with its own rock, weather and dangers.
