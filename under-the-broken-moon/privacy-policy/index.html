Under the Broken Moon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Under the Broken Moon is a single-player shelter-building game developed by Hoffmann Tech LTDA (“we”, “us”), published as MagicLab Solutions. It collects nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t connect to our servers or anyone else’s. Every picture, sound and spoken line ships inside the app.

Your shelter

Your shelters, the names you give them and your people, your progress through the sites and your settings are stored on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Links out of the game

The settings screen can open the App Store (to rate the game), your email app (to write to us) and pages on this website. What happens there is covered by those services’ own policies; if you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

Apple

If you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. They don’t identify you.

Children

Under the Broken Moon does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever begins to collect anything, such as for purchases or online features, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above before it does.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@magiclabsolutions.com.
