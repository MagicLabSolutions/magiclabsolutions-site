## Sundust: a tiny solar system to call home

**Sundust** is a cozy space program that runs on real orbital mechanics. Every planet, moon, and asteroid is truly in motion — you don't just point and fly, you swing through gravity, catch transfer windows, and learn to love the retro burn. Then you land (gently, please), step out, and get to work.

One game across **iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Android**, with full controller support, your save synced through **iCloud**, and **Game Center** achievements and leaderboards. Free to download: flying to the Moon and back costs nothing, and one in-app purchase opens the rest of the solar system. No ads, no subscription, no internet required.

---

### What you'll do

- **Fly** — real gravity, live orbits, trajectory prediction, and time warp for the long hauls.
- **Walk** — step out onto the world you landed on, sail its painted seas, and hide the HUD with Photo Mode.
- **Prospect & build** — mine ice, iron, helium-3 and more, then build rigs, depots, and refineries that keep working even while the app is closed.
- **Follow the signal** — a story across thirteen systems and ten acts, from your first wobbly liftoff to a very old mystery.
- **Make contact** — meet the Vey, walk their inhabited worlds, and race their silver saucers through Nacre's rings.
