## Giftly: never arrive empty-handed

**Giftly** remembers the people you care about and the days that matter to them — birthdays, anniversaries, the day you met, the holidays they actually celebrate — and reminds you while there is still time to do something about it.

One app across **iPhone, iPad, and Mac**, with everything synced privately through **iCloud**. No account, no ads, no tracking.

---

### What it does

- **Remembers the dates** — birthdays, anniversaries, and any day you want to add, with Feb 29 handled properly and ages that count up on their own.
- **Reminds you in time to shop** — on the day, three days, a week, or two weeks before, set per person and defaulted the way you like.
- **Keeps the ideas** — save gift ideas year-round and mark the one you chose, so the whole app stops nagging until the next occasion.
- **Suggests things that fit** — around 890 curated ideas matched to their tastes, age, relationship, and sizes, with different suggestions for children, teenagers, and adults.
- **Tracks what you gave** — a simple history so you never repeat last year's present.
- **Speaks their language** — 34 languages, with holidays that follow the country you're in.
