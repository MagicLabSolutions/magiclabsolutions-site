## Kinfold: Your family's documents, kept safe

**Kinfold** keeps your family's most important documents — passports, IDs, driver's licences, visas, and residence permits — in one private, iCloud-backed place, and gets you out the door when it's time to travel.

Scan a document and Kinfold reads it for you **on-device** — filling in the details, the photo, and the expiry date. Everything is organised by family member, tracked for renewals, and available across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

---

### Why Kinfold?

- **Everything important, in one place** — scan a passport, ID, licence, visa, or permit and Kinfold fills in the details for you, on-device. No typing, no uploading.
- **Never miss a renewal** — Kinfold tracks every expiry date and reminds you before a passport or visa lapses.
- **Smart-copy, any format** — because every value is a real type, copy an expiry date exactly how a form needs it: airline·ICAO, German, Brazil·EU, US, or ISO 8601.
- **Travel-ready** — packing checklists that adapt to the destination and travelers, with travel-readiness checks that confirm every passport outlasts the trip.
- **Private by design** — end-to-end encrypted in your iCloud, and shared only with the family members you choose.
