## Hooray: Group cards people actually remember

**Hooray** turns a birthday, farewell, or thank-you into a card the whole group signs together. Start a card for the occasion, share a private link, and everyone adds their own messages, stickers, and GIFs to a real-card spread.

When the moment arrives, the finished card lands in the recipient's inbox and opens with a confetti reveal — no app to install, works right in the browser.

### How it works

- **Start a card** — pick the occasion and recipient, choose cover art, and set the delivery date and time.
- **Share the link** — send a private signing link to friends, family, or colleagues.
- **Everyone signs** — each person adds a message in their own handwriting style and ink colour, plus stickers and GIFs.
- **Confetti reveal** — the card is delivered by email at the scheduled moment and opens with a celebration.
