## Pauta: one headline a day. The rest is a footnote.

**Pauta** is a daily planner for one person and their family, with the identity of a printed
newspaper. Every day opens with a single headline — the thing that actually matters — and the rest
falls below the fold, where it belongs.

Coming soon to **iPhone, iPad and Mac**, in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

---

### What it does

- **Capture by voice or text** — say it the way it comes out ("tomorrow buy milk") and an
  assistant works out the day and the project, and files it there.
- **A shared household** — tasks, the grocery list, and a wish list whose items climb in priority
  the longer they wait.
- **Projects by area** — Home, Work, Personal and Family, each with a roadmap of milestones.
- **An ideas notebook** — ideas live for 30 days; act on one or let it fade, with no guilt
  attached.
- **Scheduled prompts** — questions that run on their own and come back with proposals you accept
  or ignore. Nothing becomes data until you say so.
