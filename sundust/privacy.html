Sundust — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Sundust is a single-player game developed by Hoffmann Tech LTDA (“we”, “us”). We built Sundust to be played entirely offline, and to collect as little as possible.

We don’t collect your data

Sundust does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs.

Your game saves

Your progress is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your save is also synced through Apple’s iCloud so it follows you across your Apple devices. This data lives in your own private iCloud storage — we cannot see or access it — and is handled under Apple’s iCloud terms and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you play with Game Center enabled, your leaderboard scores and achievements are handled by Apple’s Game Center and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, not ours. You can turn Game Center off in your device Settings at any time.

Children

Sundust does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above.
